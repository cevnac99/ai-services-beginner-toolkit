# Community Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and useful.

- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Welcome beginner questions without ridicule.
- Do not publish private information or pressure anyone to share account access.
- Do not use the project to promote deceptive earnings claims, spam, plagiarism, or unsafe automation.
- Report a concern privately through the support route in `SUPPORT.md`.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that break these rules and may restrict repeated abusive participation.
