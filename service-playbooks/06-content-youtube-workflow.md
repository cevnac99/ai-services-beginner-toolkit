# Content and YouTube Workflow

## The simple offer

Help a creator or marketing team turn one original source into reviewed drafts for a video, short clips, captions, and posts without automating editorial approval or publishing.

## Who may buy it

YouTube creators, podcasters, educators, consultants, and small marketing teams with original long-form material.

## The repeated problem

Useful ideas remain trapped in recordings or notes because turning them into several formats takes time and repeated review.

## Build this first

A fictional production board that turns one owner-created source into:

- video angle and outline;
- three short-form hooks;
- one carousel outline;
- caption drafts;
- source and rights checklist;
- accuracy and approval status.

## Five fictional test cases

1. Original transcript with clear ownership and topic.
2. Missing source or unclear quote attribution.
3. Conflicting facts in the source notes.
4. Request to reuse copyrighted material without permission.
5. Sensitive claim, impersonation, disclosure, or platform-policy concern.

## Copyable agent prompt

```text
Create a fictional content-repurposing workflow for a [CREATOR OR BUSINESS TYPE].

Use only owner-created source material supplied for the demonstration. Produce a video outline, three short hooks, one carousel outline, caption drafts, and a review checklist.

Rules:
- do not copy third-party scripts, footage, thumbnails, voices, or music;
- do not invent quotes, sources, credentials, statistics, or results;
- mark every uncertain fact for review;
- do not upload, schedule, publish, or access external accounts;
- keep rights, accuracy, disclosure, and final editorial approval human-controlled.

Test normal, missing-source, conflicting-fact, rights-risk, and sensitive-claim cases. Done when every output traces back to the approved source and cannot publish by itself.
```

## Questions for a possible buyer

- Which original source should create the weekly content?
- Who owns every recording, image, clip, voice, and music track?
- Which facts or claims require a source?
- Who gives final editorial approval?
- Which channel and format matter most this month?

## What you can deliver

A production board, reusable briefs, source checklist, review stages, and a sample weekly content pack created from fictional or owner-approved material.

## Human-controlled boundary

People control rights, facts, disclosures, brand voice, platform accounts, uploads, publication, comments, and monetization decisions.

## Next move

Use one short owner-created article or transcript and produce a complete draft pack without publishing it. Ask the creator which output saves the most review time.
